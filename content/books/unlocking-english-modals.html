Sample Exam Questions & Analysis

Question 1 (Need Not Auxiliary Trap)

You need not to bring (A) / any expensive reference manuals (B) / for tomorrow's revision workshop. (C) / No error (D)

Error in Part (A): Remove to.
Examiner Trap Explained: When need is used as a modal auxiliary in the negative (need not), it is followed by a bare infinitive (bring), never a full infinitive with to.
Question 2 (Modal Perfect Deduction)

The lights were off and nobody answered the door; they ______ left early for the station. (A) must have (B) should have (C) could (D) will have

Correct Answer: (A) must have
Rule: Must have + V3 expresses a strong logical deduction about a past event based on solid evidence.
