Sample Exam Questions & Analysis

Question 1 (SSC CGL Cloze Test Trap)

The panel members discussed about the financial crisis (A) / for nearly four hours (B) / before reaching a consensus. (C) / No error (D)

Error in Part (A): Remove about.
Examiner Trap Explained: The transitive verb discuss directly governs its object (the financial crisis). Adding about is a widespread spoken colloquial redundancy penalized in competitive tests.
Question 2 (Fixed Preposition Nuance)

Our company deals ______ organic herbal cosmetics and exports to thirty countries. (A) with (B) in (C) about (D) on

Correct Answer: (B) in
Rule: Deal in means to do trade or business in a commodity. Deal with means to handle a person, problem, or topic.
