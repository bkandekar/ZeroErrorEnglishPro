Sample Exam Questions & Analysis

Shortcut #14 (Correlative Conjunction Trap)

No sooner had the alarm sounded (A) / when the security guards rushed (B) / towards the main perimeter gate. (C) / No error (D)

Error in Part (B): Replace when with than.
The Shortcut: No sooner is a comparative construction and takes strictly than. Hardly / Scarcely take when.
Shortcut #47 (Latin Comparative Trap)

This customized laptop model is superior than (A) / the one manufactured by the competitor (B) / in terms of battery efficiency. (C) / No error (D)

Error in Part (A): Replace than with to.
The Shortcut: Latin comparatives (superior, inferior, junior, senior, prior, anterior, posterior) take the preposition to, never than.
