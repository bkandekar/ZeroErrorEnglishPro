Sample Exam Questions & Analysis

Question 1 (SSC CGL 2023)

The chief engineer has submitted the final project feasibility report (A) / to the municipal commissioner (B) / two weeks ago. (C) / No error (D)

Error in Part (A): Replace has submitted with submitted.
Examiner Trap Explained: The adverbial time phrase two weeks ago specifies a closed, completed historical moment in the past. Present Perfect cannot combine with past-specific time markers.
Question 2 (Banking PO Pattern)

If the authorities had taken precautionary measures in time, (A) / the catastrophic flooding in the lower valley (B) / could have been avoided. (C) / No error (D)

Result: (D) No error.
Analysis: This tests Third Conditional syntax: If + past perfect (had taken) paired with modal perfect passive (could have been avoided) in the main result clause.
