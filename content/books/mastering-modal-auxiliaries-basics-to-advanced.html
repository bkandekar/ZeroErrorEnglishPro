Sample Exam Questions & Analysis

Question 1 (Would Rather Bare Infinitive Trap)

I would rather to stay back at the accommodation (A) / than to venture outside in this torrential downpour (B) / without an umbrella. (C) / No error (D)

Error in Part (A) and (B): Remove to in both clauses (write would rather stay... than venture...).
Rule: The semi-modal phrase would rather is followed strictly by a bare infinitive, and elements joined by than must maintain parallel structure.
Question 2 (Epistemic Deduction in Reading)

Given that the monetary policy committee unanimously voted against an interest rate reduction, inflation projections ______ remained stubbornly above target. (A) must have (B) should have (C) may not have (D) could not have

Correct Answer: (A) must have
Analysis: Based on the factual premise of unanimous opposition, must have represents the logical necessity/deduction of persistent inflationary pressure.
