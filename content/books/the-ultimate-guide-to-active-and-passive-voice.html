Sample Exam Questions & Analysis

Question 1 (Quasi-Passive Exam Pattern)

Active: Quinine tastes bitter.

Correct Passive Form: Quinine is bitter when it is tasted.
Examiner Trap Explained: Verbs of sensation do not take direct objects; their passive requires a temporal clause with past participle.
Question 2 (Preposition Retention Trap)

Active: The crowd laughed at the clown.

Correct Passive Form: The clown was laughed at by the crowd.
Examiner Trap Explained: Options routinely drop at ("The clown was laughed by the crowd"), which makes the sentence ungrammatical. The preposition attached to the phrasal verb must be strictly preserved.
