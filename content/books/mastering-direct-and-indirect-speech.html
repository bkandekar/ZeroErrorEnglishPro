Sample Exam Questions & Analysis

Question 1 (SSC CGL Tier-2 Actual Exam)

The teacher said to the students, "The earth revolves around the sun."

Correct Indirect Form: The teacher told the students that the earth revolves around the sun.
Examiner Trap Explained: Students often mistakenly backshift revolves into revolved. Because this is a permanent cosmic/scientific fact, the present tense is strictly retained.
Question 2 (Interrogative Pattern)

He said to me, "Where do you live?"

Correct Indirect Form: He asked me where I lived.
Examiner Trap Explained: Candidates frequently write "where did I live" retaining question word order, or add an incorrect that ("asked me that where..."). In indirect speech, the clause becomes an affirmative statement.
