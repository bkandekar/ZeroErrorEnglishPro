When an examiner wants to separate 99th percentile candidates from the rest of the pack in SSC CGL Tier 2 or IBPS PO Mains, they introduce a sentence featuring a possessive case before a gerund or an unattached dangling participle.

Sample Exam Questions & Analysis

Question 1 (Gerund Possessive Rule)

The management objected to him arriving late (A) / at every critical board meeting (B) / without prior intimation. (C) / No error (D)

Error in Part (A): Replace him with his.
Examiner Trap Explained: Arriving functions here as a gerund (verbal noun). A noun or pronoun modifying a gerund must be in the possessive case (his arriving).
Question 2 (Dangling Participle Trap)

Walking along the riverbank on Sunday morning, (A) / a venomous snake bit him (B) / on his right ankle. (C) / No error (D)

Error in Part (A): The participial phrase lacks an appropriate logical subject.
Correction: While he was walking along the riverbank... As written, the sentence implies that the snake was walking along the riverbank.
