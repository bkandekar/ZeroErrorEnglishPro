Sample Exam Questions & Analysis

Question 1 (Comma Splice Trap)

The research proposal was delayed for several weeks (A) , however, the committee eventually approved (B) / the funding allocation. (C) / No error (D)

Error in Part (A/B): A comma cannot link two independent clauses before however.
Correction: Use a semicolon: ...delayed for several weeks; however, the committee...
Question 2 (Apostrophe Possessive Rule)

The dog wagged it's tail enthusiastically (A) / when its owner returned (B) / from the overseas trip. (C) / No error (D)

Error in Part (A): Replace it's with its.
Rule: It's is a contraction for it is or it has. The possessive determiner has NO apostrophe (its tail).
