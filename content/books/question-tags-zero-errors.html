However, in competitive examinations like SSC CGL, CHSL, and Railway RRB NTPC, examiners never ask basic questions. Sample Exam Questions & Analysis

Question 1 (SSC CHSL Actual Exam)

He seldom visits his native village nowadays, ______? (A) doesn't he? (B) does he? (C) isn't it? (D) didn't he?

Correct Answer: (B) does he?
Examiner Trap Explained: Because seldom is inherently negative in meaning, the sentence is semantically negative. Adding a negative tag (doesn't he?) creates an erroneous double negative.
Question 2 (Indefinite Pronoun Trap)

Everybody was present at the inauguration ceremony, ______? (A) wasn't he? (B) weren't they? (C) wasn't they? (D) didn't they?

Correct Answer: (B) weren't they?
Rule: While everybody takes a singular verb in the declarative clause (was), its question tag pronoun is always they, which syntactically demands the plural auxiliary weren't.
