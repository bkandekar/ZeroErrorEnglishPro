About the Book

Articles (A, An, The) are the most deceptively simple words in English, yet they trigger high rates of negative markings in SSC CGL error spotting and cloze tests. Why? Because Indian languages have no grammatical equivalent to English articles, leaving aspirants reliant on flawed auditory instinct.

Prepared specifically for the latest TCS exam syllabus by an educator with 22 years of teaching and 15+ years of competitive coaching, this manual delivers complete precision on phonetic sound rules (vowel sound vs vowel letter), geographic naming rules, and zero-article omissions.

Sample Exam Questions & Analysis

Question 1 (Primary Purpose Omission Trap)

After the severe road accident, (A) / the injured driver was immediately rushed (B) / to the hospital. (C) / No error (D)

Error in Part (C): Remove the (write to hospital).
Examiner Trap Explained: When words like school, college, church, hospital, prison, market are visited for their primary fundamental purpose (medical treatment for an injured patient), no article is used.
Question 2 (Geographic Mountain Trap)

The Mount Everest is (A) / the highest mountain peak in the world (B) / and attracts mountaineers worldwide. (C) / No error (D)

Error in Part (A): Remove The (write Mount Everest is...).
Rule: While mountain ranges take the (the Himalayas, the Alps), individual solitary mountain peaks (Mount Everest, Mount Abu, Kanchenjunga) do NOT take the definite article.
