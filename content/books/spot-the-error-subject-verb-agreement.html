Sample Exam Questions & Analysis

Question 1 (SSC CGL Pattern)

The quality of the freshly harvested organic apples exported to several European countries (A) / are exceptionally high (B) / according to the international inspection committee. (C) / No error (D)

Error in Part (B): Replace are with is.
Examiner Trap Explained: The plural noun apples and countries immediately precede the verb, tempting the candidate into an auditory proximity error. The true subject is the singular abstract noun quality.
Question 2 (IBPS PO Pattern)

He is one of those dedicated research scholars who (A) / has worked tirelessly on the vaccine trial (B) / without seeking any commercial publicity. (C) / No error (D)

Error in Part (B): Replace has worked with have worked.
Examiner Trap Explained: In the construction one of those + plural noun + who/that, the relative pronoun refers back to the plural antecedent scholars, requiring a plural verb.
