Sample Exam Questions & Analysis

Question 1 (Lest Trap in SSC CGL)

Walk carefully lest you (A) / should not trip and fall (B) / on the slippery cobblestones. (C) / No error (D)

Error in Part (B): Remove not (write should trip and fall).
Examiner Trap Explained: Lest is inherently negative (meaning "for fear that"). Using not creates a redundant double negative. Furthermore, lest is strictly followed by should (or a subjunctive base verb).
Question 2 (Needn't Have Nuance)

You ______ brought an umbrella because it didn't rain at all. (A) needn't have (B) didn't need to (C) couldn't have (D) wouldn't have

Correct Answer: (A) needn't have
Rule: Needn't have + V3 indicates that an action was completed, but in hindsight, it was unnecessary.
