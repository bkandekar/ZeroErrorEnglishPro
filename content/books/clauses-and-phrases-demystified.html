Sample Exam Questions & Analysis

Question 1 (Relative Pronoun Case Trap)

The candidate whom they believed was the most capable (A) / failed to answer the technical questions (B) / during the final interview. (C) / No error (D)

Error in Part (A): Replace whom with who.
Examiner Trap Explained: The parenthetical clause they believed distracts candidates into thinking the pronoun is an object. In reality, the pronoun is the subject of the finite verb was (who was the most capable).
Question 2 (Adverbial Correlative Trap)

Although he prepared meticulously for the entrance test, (A) / but he could not secure (B) / a seat in the premier institute. (C) / No error (D)

Error in Part (B): Remove but (or replace with yet).
Rule: In standard formal English, the subordinating conjunction although / though cannot be paired with the coordinating conjunction but in the main clause.
