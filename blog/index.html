The Superfluous Preposition Trap

Many transitive verbs take direct objects and become ungrammatical when candidates insert conversational prepositions like 'about', 'for', or 'into'.

Incorrect: The committee discussed about the economic plan.
Correct: The committee discussed the economic plan.
