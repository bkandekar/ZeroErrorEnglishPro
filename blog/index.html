Active & Passive Voice: How to Handle Quasi-Passive & Sensory Verbs

Never lose marks on verbs like 'taste', 'smell', and 'feel'. Master the exact mathematical formula for quasi-passive conversions.

Core Governing Rule Formula:
Subject + be + adjective + when it is/was + V3

Understanding Middle and Quasi-Passive Verbs

Certain active verbs have passive meanings without having an obvious direct object. For instance: 'The rose smells sweet' or 'Quinine tastes bitter'.

Exam Blueprint:
Quinine tastes bitter -> Quinine is bitter when it is tasted.
