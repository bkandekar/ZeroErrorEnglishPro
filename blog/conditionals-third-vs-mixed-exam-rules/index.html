Third Conditionals vs Mixed Conditionals: Eliminating Guesswork

Understand unfulfilled past regrets vs. ongoing present consequences with foolproof clause balance formulas.

Core Governing Rule Formula:
If + had + V3 (Past Regret) -> would have + V3 (Past Outcome)

The Classic Third Conditional Formula

When discussing hypothetical situations in the past that did not occur, both the condition clause and the result clause must remain in the past modal domain.

Structure: If + had + V3, Subject + would have + V3.
Example: If he had prepared thoroughly, he would have cleared the exam.
